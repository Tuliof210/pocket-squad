# Learnings — Pocket Squad

Durable, one-line rules. Appended by /ps:publish after each merge; read by
/ps:story during refinement, and by /ps:run and /ps:review as binding norms. A
rule must change future behavior — otherwise delete it. Cap: 30 rules; when adding past the cap, delete the weakest one.

Format: `- [scope] imperative rule (added YYYY-MM-DD)`
